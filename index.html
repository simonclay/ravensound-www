About Raven Sound

Sound engineered by a musician, for musicians

Raven Sound is run by Jamie Stallwood. Based in the South East of England, I’m an experienced freelance sound engineer with a lifelong passion for music. Having played drums since the age of four, I understand music from both sides of the stage: not just how a great mix should sound, but how important it is for musicians to feel heard, supported and confident in their performance.

Since 2022, I’ve been the trusted in-house sound engineer for bands performing at the Dashwood Roadhouse, working across a wide range of live music genres and line-ups. This experience has built a reputation for reliable, professional sound and the attention to detail that comes from being a musician myself.

Services

  1. 01

    Live Sound & PA Hire

    Raven Sound provides professional live sound engineering at affordable prices for a variety of venues and events, from local pub gigs and private parties to weddings and corporate events.

    Full PA system hire and operation is available where required.

    Please note: Raven Sound provides PA hire as part of its sound engineering service, but equipment is not available for dry hire. I supply and operate the equipment myself to ensure everything is properly managed and sounding its best.

  2. 02

    Recording & Mixing

    Want your live performance to sound as good online as it did on stage?

    I can record directly through my sound desk and mix the resulting audio in my home studio, or work with an existing recording supplied by you.

    I offer a range of recording and post-production services, including:

    • One stereo track (live mix of the night)
    • Multi-track (raw non-mixed individual stems)
    • Post-gig editing / mixing of the whole gig as a high-quality demo

    Whether you’re looking to promote your band, share a live performance, or create a demo, Raven Sound can help turn your recordings into polished audio.

  3. 03

    Drum Dep Services

    As a highly experienced drummer, I also offer drum dep services, stepping in when a band’s regular drummer is unavailable. Whether it’s a one-off gig, a short run of shows, or last-minute cover, I can help you keep the show on the road.

  4. 04

    Stage & Lighting

    Raven Sound does not currently provide stage building or stage lighting as standalone services. However, I have connections with trusted professionals who can provide these services and I can contact them on your behalf, helping to bring together the different elements needed for your event.

  5. 05

    Professional & Insured

    Raven Sound carries full Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance, giving you peace of mind that your event and sound requirements are in professional hands.

Whether your project is big or small, Raven Sound is focused on one thing: making your music sound its best.
