Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They can be used to remember you, or to measure how a website is used.

Your choice

When you first visit this website, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Nothing that sets cookies is loaded until you choose. If you click Reject, no cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you change to Reject, the cookies listed below are removed.

Cookies we use

Only if you click Accept, we use Google Analytics to understand how people use the website, such as which pages are visited and how people found us, so we can improve it. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: tells one visitor apart from another. Expires after 2 years.
  • ga[ID]: keeps track of your visit. Expires after 2 years.

The information is collected by Google on our behalf. We see it as overall statistics and can’t use it to identify you. Google’s own privacy policy explains how it handles this information: policies.google.com/privacy.

We don’t use advertising cookies.

Other things on this website

Everything you see and hear on this website, including the fonts, photos and audio samples, comes from the website itself rather than from other companies’ servers, and sets no cookies.

When you press Send message on the contact form, your message is passed to Web3Forms, which emails it to us. This doesn’t set any cookies. See our Privacy Policy for how we look after what you send us.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. For help, visit aboutcookies.org.
