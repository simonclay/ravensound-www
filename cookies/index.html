Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files that some websites store on your device to remember you, measure visits or show you adverts.

This website doesn’t use cookies

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, so nothing is stored on your device when you visit, and there’s no cookie banner to click through.

Everything you see and hear on this website, including the fonts, photos and audio samples, comes from the website itself rather than from other companies’ servers.

The contact form

When you press Send message, your message is passed to Web3Forms, which emails it to us. This doesn’t set any cookies on this website. See our Privacy Policy for how we look after what you send us.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that uses cookies, such as visitor statistics, we’ll update this page and ask for your permission before setting any.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies from any website in your browser settings. For help, visit aboutcookies.org.
